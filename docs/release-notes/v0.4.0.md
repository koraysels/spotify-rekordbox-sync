Download missing tracks with spotDL, plus buy and search links for everything you still need.

## What's new in 0.4.0

### Download missing tracks (optional, off by default)

Switch it on in **Settings → Downloads**. rbsync fetches spotDL 4.6.0 once
(checked against a pinned SHA-256) and finds ffmpeg on your system.

- **Download N missing** on a playlist, or right-click a missing track →
  **Download**. Nothing downloads without you pressing a button.
- Files land in `~/Downloads/rbsync/<playlist>/` (folder changeable), so you
  can drag them into Apple Music or rekordbox your own way. rbsync never
  writes downloads into rekordbox's database.
- A track already downloaded is never fetched twice.
- Progress, cancel and resume in the **Downloads** panel. A paused queue
  (rate limit, missing tool, unplugged drive) shows on the main screen with
  the reason and a Resume button.

**Quality, honestly.** The audio comes from YouTube Music: Opus around
130–160 kbps without Premium. rbsync converts it once to a CBR mp3 at a
bitrate matched to the source (the next step above 1.25× the source, at most
256 kbps), so the file is not inflated to a fake 320. Every download is
labelled `lowq` in its comment, so you always see what still needs replacing.
Fine for practice and emergencies; buy the track for club use.

**Metadata from Spotify.** Title, artists, album, album artist, release date,
track and disc number, label, ISRC, cover and the Spotify link are written as
proper ID3 tags. The ISRC means the track matches immediately once it is in
your rekordbox collection. BPM and key are added when rbsync already has them.

Against Spotify rate limits you can enter your own Spotify Client ID and
secret for spotDL; the secret is never shown again or logged. Intel Macs need
spotDL installed with `pipx install spotdl` or `brew install spotdl`.

### Buy and search links

Right-click a missing track, or a matched track labelled `lowq`, for **Find
on** Beatport, Bandcamp, Traxsource, Juno Download and Discogs. The track
info panel has the same links. These work whether or not downloads are on.
Soulseek stays as before: **Copy "Artist - Title"** and paste.
